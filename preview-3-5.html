The headline changes

A broader time workspace, without losing the timer at its center.

The 3.5 preview combines large new capabilities with practical improvements to the tasks, reports, sounds, and offline workflow already in XTimers.

World Calendar

See dates in their wider context.

Move through month, week, and year views with an offline 2026–2027 holiday catalog covering 871 country, territory, and regional profiles, Chinese lunisolar dates, festivals, and historical anniversaries.

Optional global-content layers can require a download, and coverage varies by profile.

Personal Calendar

Your events stay on your Mac.

With permission, selected Apple calendars can appear as a read-only, on-device overlay. XTimers does not upload, create, edit, or delete those events.

Calendar selection for display is separate from Event Interrupt selection.

Single + Minimal

Two quieter ways to focus.

Single and Minimal views join the existing List and Focus views. Step between tasks, keep a large reading in sight, remember a separate size for each view, and pin the main window when needed.

Space can start, pause, or resume the selected task when that action is available.

Alarms + Events

Stay on schedule with honest readiness.

Create one-time or weekly alarms and choose whether each joined device should ring; the creating device starts enabled. Event Interrupts can show silent, full-screen visual reminders 1, 2, 5, 10, or 15 minutes before selected calendar events.

On Mac, alarms require the Mac to be awake and XTimers or its notification service available. Event Interrupts require XTimers running.

Sound Library

Find the right sound instead of hunting for it.

Browse 66 sounds: 30 Classic Tones, 22 World Ceremonial sounds, and 14 XTimers Originals. Search, use Favorites and Recents, preview waveforms, and keep imported sounds in My Sounds.

The Mac alarm editor does not offer a user-selected alarm sound in this preview.

Appearance

Make the timer surface your own.

Choose System, Light, Dark, or pure-black styling, then add colors, local photos, six bundled backgrounds, additional downloadable choices, or a local folder that rotates daily.

Photo controls include adaptive or manual dimming, title-bar coverage, and automatic or custom task text colors.

Completion

Move naturally into the next session.

Actionable completion cards bring Stop Sound, Add Note, Restart, and Done together. Eligible completed tasks can resume or restart now or later; fixed-end tasks can choose a new end time.

Completion cards stack, can be moved, and keep the relevant task within reach.

Archives

Find past work and report on it faster.

Search grouped archives, distinguish reset and archived snapshots, and select multiple archives when building a report. Existing CSV, HTML, and PDF export options remain available.

Deleted synchronized archives are designed not to reappear after sync.

Optional protection

Sync with clearer boundaries.

Local-write-first behavior is designed to keep ordinary timer work responsive through temporary connection loss. Eligible signed-in accounts can also choose Protected Sync for newly synchronized supported content.

Protected after activationNewly synced supported non-reminder content and supported assets are encrypted on the Mac before synchronization.
Outside protectionReminder content, account and sync metadata, content synced before activation, and on-device storage remain outside this protection.
Recovery mattersSave the recovery code securely. Losing both Keychain access and the code can make protected content unrecoverable.
